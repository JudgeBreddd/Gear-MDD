# Gear MDD

Calculator for the 18-month inspection maintenance due date (MDD) on gear, built to run on a phone.

**Open it:** https://judgebreddd.github.io/Gear-MDD/

## What it does
- Enter serial number, nomenclature, 18-month completion date, and preservation / de-preservation dates.
- The 547-day interval only counts while gear is out of preservation. Leap years are handled automatically.
- Level 2 prez lasts 365 days. Gear stays in prez if the 365-day re-prez was done (the default); otherwise the clock restarts at day 365. Level 3 never drops out.
- Shows 28-day DSI due dates for gear in prez (for reference; they don't change the MDD).
- Reports as PDF or CSV. Settings let you change the interval, Level 2 length, DSI interval, date format and more.

## Install on a phone
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

It works without signal after the first visit.

## Where your data is saved
Everything you enter is saved only in the browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to GitHub or anywhere else,
and each person's gear list is separate. Clearing browser data erases it, so use **Settings > Export backup** now and then.
To move your list to another device, export a backup there and use **Import backup**.
